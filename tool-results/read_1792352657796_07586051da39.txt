    38→12.1.1 Goal of Optimization
    39→Although optimization provides a way to minimize the loss function for deep learning,
    40→in essence, the goals of optimization and deep learning are fundamentally different. The
    41→former is primarily concerned with minimizing an objective whereas the latter is concerned
    42→with finding a suitable model, given a finite amount of data. In Section 3.6, we discussed the
    43→difference between these two goals in detail. For instance, training error and generalization
    44→error generally differ: since the objective function of the optimization algorithm is usually a
    45→loss function based on the training dataset, the goal of optimization is to reduce the training
    46→error. However, the goal of deep learning (or more broadly, statistical inference) is to reduce
    47→the generalization error. To accomplish the latter we need to pay attention to overfitting in
    48→addition to using the optimization algorithm to reduce the training error.
